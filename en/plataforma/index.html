One single source of information

When the whole home works connected, care and management become simpler

In many care settings, information is scattered across apps, spreadsheets, documents, phone calls and informal staff knowledge. OmiCare unifies records, operations and communication so the whole team works from shared, up-to-date information.

  • Unified information

    Staff consult one shared, up-to-date record without rebuilding history at every handover.

  • Coordinated work

    Alerts, tasks and processes connect information with each professional's actions.

  • Traceability

    Every relevant action is logged and can be reviewed when needed.

  • Global view

    Management understands what happens across one or several sites with reliable indicators and logs.

How OmiCare works day to day

Modules are not isolated: they connect information, people and processes

Example: during a shift handover, the team checks open incidents, reviews pending tasks and logs actions taken before continuing the day.

  1. 1

    Detect

    At handover, a pending incident or relevant observation is visible to incoming staff without relying on calls or paper.

  2. 2

    Notify

    OmiCare surfaces the alert or update to the right people according to role and responsibility.

  3. 3

    Act

    The team receives a task, follows a process or logs the intervention directly in the platform.

  4. 4

    Supervise

    Clinical leads and management check status, timings and open incidents.

  5. 5

    Demonstrate

    Recorded actions remain available for follow-up, quality or audit preparation.

OmiCare does not just store data: it connects information, people and processes so every situation is followed through.

Multi-site and integrations

Ready to grow from one home to a care group

OmiCare separates organisations and sites, adapts roles and permissions to each responsibility and offers a consolidated view for groups managing several homes. Information can be exported and integrated with other systems under service agreements.

  • Separate organisations and sites

    Each home keeps its environment with role-based permissions.

  • Consolidated view

    Group management accesses indicators and reports across several sites.

  • Open APIs

    Integrations with identity, ERP, laboratory or other provider systems.

  • European infrastructure

    Data hosted in European Union regions.

Security and compliance

Security and traceability by design

OmiCare incorporates security and information protection controls oriented to the care sector and is working on conformity with the National Security Framework (ENS) at MEDIUM category.

Access and permissions

  • Configurable roles by responsibility
  • Separation by organisations and sites
  • Data access according to profile, role and site

Information protection

  • Encryption in transit via TLS
  • AES-256-GCM encryption of sensitive data at rest
  • Primary infrastructure in the European Union

Traceability

  • Logging of relevant platform actions
  • Activity tracking for quality and audit
  • Exportable evidence according to site permissions

Conformity and good practice

  • ENS MEDIUM category: conformity process in progress
  • GDPR: controls and processes oriented to compliance
  • Controls aligned with information security good practice
