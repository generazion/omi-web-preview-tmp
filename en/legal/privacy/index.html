Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 3, 2026

Draft for review. Must be validated by legal counsel before production launch.

1. Data controller

OmiCare is the data controller for personal data collected through this website.

  • Contact email: info@omicare.tech
  • Data Protection Officer: dpo@omicare.tech

2. Purposes

We process your data to:

  1. Reply to information requests, demos and commercial proposals.
  2. Manage newsletter subscriptions (with explicit consent).
  3. Improve the website experience via aggregated and anonymous analytics.
  4. Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Legal basis

  • Consent (Art. 6.1.a GDPR) for newsletter and analytics cookies.
  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) for handling commercial requests.
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6.1.c GDPR) for record retention.

4. Retention

Data is kept while the relationship is active or until the data subject requests deletion.

5. Recipients

We do not transfer data to third parties unless legally required or to processors operating under contract on behalf of OmiCare. All production infrastructure is hosted in the European Union (Cloud Run, europe-southwest1).

6. Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to dpo@omicare.tech. You may also lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

7. Security

We apply ENS Medium category level and ISO 27001 controls, including AES-256-GCM encryption, granular access control and immutable audit logs.

8. Cookies

See the Cookie Policy for details on how to manage your preferences.
